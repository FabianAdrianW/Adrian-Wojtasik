# Prompty do Google Flow: logo z bramą torii · Adrian Wojtasik

**Ustawienia Flow:** Obraz · 1:1 · Nano Banana Pro · x4 (cztery warianty naraz).

**Zasady dla wszystkich promptów:**
- Bez napisów w obrazie. Modele źle piszą litery, a nazwisko dodam szeryfem Sawarabi Mincho w SVG.
- Płaski znak na jednolitym tle. Wynik z Flow to kierunek: wybrany wariant przerysowuję jako czysty wektor SVG, żeby był ostry w każdym rozmiarze, od favicony po wizytówkę.
- Nigdy czerwona brama. Kolory marki: Sumi #121311, Washi #F2EFE7, Lampion #FFB547 (światło), Mech #33503F.

Obecny roboczy znak na stronie: geometryczna torii, poprzeczka **nuki** świeci bursztynem jak linia LED.

---

## L1 · Minimalna torii z linią światła (rekomendacja)
```
Minimalist flat vector logo mark of a Japanese torii gate, drawn with clean geometric shapes and precise thin proportions, the top beam (kasagi) gently curved upward at both ends, two straight pillars, the lower crossbeam (nuki) is a glowing warm amber LED light line (#FFB547) with a soft subtle glow, the rest of the gate in off-white (#F2EFE7), centered on a solid deep black background (#121311), modern tech brand identity, balanced negative space, no text, no letters, no gradients except the soft glow, no red color, no shadows, no 3D, aspect ratio 1:1
```

## L2 · Torii jako znak z linii (monoline)
```
Monoline logo icon of a Japanese torii gate made from a single continuous thin line of uniform stroke width, geometric and minimal, the horizontal crossbeam rendered as a glowing amber neon line (#FFB547), the rest of the line in off-white (#F2EFE7), on a solid deep black background (#121311), premium modern technology studio identity, perfectly symmetrical, lots of empty space, no text, no letters, no red, no 3D, flat vector style, aspect ratio 1:1
```

## L3 · Torii w kwadracie (sygnet jak pieczęć hanko)
```
Square seal logo mark inspired by a Japanese hanko stamp, a minimal geometric torii gate cut out as negative space inside a solid deep moss green square (#33503F) with slightly rounded corners, the lower crossbeam of the gate filled with warm amber light (#FFB547), clean flat vector, precise modern proportions, on a plain off-white background (#F2EFE7), no text, no letters, no red, no texture, no 3D, aspect ratio 1:1
```

## L4 · Torii z obwodem elektronicznym (najbardziej „tech”)
```
Minimal flat vector logo of a Japanese torii gate where the two pillars continue downward as thin circuit board traces ending in small round nodes, the crossbeam glowing warm amber (#FFB547) like an LED, the gate in off-white (#F2EFE7), on a solid deep black background (#121311), elegant, sparse and geometric, modern technology brand, no text, no letters, no red, no busy details, no 3D, aspect ratio 1:1
```

## L5 · Torii ze schodzącym światłem (wersja do druku na jasnym)
```
Flat vector logo mark of a minimal geometric Japanese torii gate in solid ink black (#121311) on a plain warm off-white paper background (#F2EFE7), the lower crossbeam in deep amber (#8A5A12), crisp edges, refined proportions inspired by modern Japanese graphic design, symmetrical, generous empty space, no text, no letters, no red, no glow, no shadows, no 3D, aspect ratio 1:1
```
